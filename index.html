Everything on the menu here is vegetarian — some of it Jain, all of it generous.

The Saffron Saga runs an unlimited thali alongside an à la carte spread, so a table can mix a slow-cooked curry with a plate of Schezwan noodles without anyone having to compromise. The terrace stays open late for anyone who'd rather eat under the sky.

  • All-you-can-eat thali service
  • Open-air terrace seating
  • 100% vegetarian kitchen, with vegan dishes marked on request
  • Jain-style preparation available for most dishes
Guests often mention the classy, unhurried ambience and how quickly the food arrives even on a full terrace — the veg crispy starter and the biryani come up again and again in write-ups. — a paraphrase of recurring themes across Google reviews
